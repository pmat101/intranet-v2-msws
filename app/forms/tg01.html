TECHNICAL GUIDELINES / TG01

Chat Space Guidelines

Guidelines for project communication, coordination and information sharing through Chat Space.

Guidelines

12 POINTS
01

What is Chat Space used for?

Chat Space is used for project-related communication, coordination and sharing of information among team members.

02

Who creates the group?

The concerned project team creates the Chat Space for the project and adds the required members.

03

What should be the naming format?

Use the project-related name so that the Chat Space can be easily identified by everyone.

04

What should be included in the project description?

The project description should provide the relevant project information required by the team.

05

Who can edit the description?

The designated members responsible for managing the Chat Space can update its description when required.

06

Who should be added as members?

Only the concerned project team members and other required stakeholders should be added.

07

How should the team interact?

Project discussions, updates and important communication should be kept within the relevant Chat Space.

08

Can documents be shared?

Yes. Relevant project documents can be shared in the Chat Space for easy access by the concerned team.

09

How are tasks handled?

Tasks and project activities can be communicated and coordinated through the Chat Space.

10

What about notification settings?

Members can manage notification settings according to their project communication requirements.

11

How can messages be searched?

Use the search option available in Chat Space to find previous messages and shared information.

12

What if someone leaves the Space?

The concerned member can rejoin the relevant Space when access is required.
